The Problem With Traditional Solar

The solar industry has a fundamental flaw that costs homeowners and businesses billions in lost energy every year. Fixed solar panels are installed at a single angle and never move — meaning they only capture peak sunlight for a fraction of the day. As the sun travels across the sky from east to west, fixed panels increasingly miss the direct rays that generate the most electricity. Add to this the problem of overheating — solar panels actually lose efficiency as they get hotter — and you have a system that is far less effective than the technology promises.

Existing solar tracking systems do exist, but they cost upwards of £15,000, require professional installation, heavy foundations, and complex maintenance contracts. They are simply out of reach for the average homeowner, small business, or off-grid enthusiast. Until now.

Grid-Tie Technology Explained

The SGT is fully designed for grid-tie operation — meaning your system connects directly to the national electricity grid through a compatible inverter. This is the most financially rewarding way to use solar in the UK, and the SGT makes it more accessible than ever before.

Under the UK Government's Smart Export Guarantee (SEG) scheme, your energy supplier is legally required to pay you for every kilowatt-hour of electricity you export to the grid. Combined with the 60–80% more energy the SGT generates compared to fixed panels, the financial case for investing is compelling.

⚡ How Grid-Tie Works

Your SGT generates DC electricity from sunlight. A grid-tie inverter converts this to AC electricity matching the grid frequency. Excess power flows to the grid automatically — and you get paid for it.

💷 Smart Export Guarantee

UK energy suppliers pay between 4p and 15p per kWh exported. With the SGT generating up to 80% more than fixed panels, your SEG earnings are proportionally higher than any fixed-panel system.

🔋 Battery Storage Option

Pair your SGT with a battery storage system to store daytime generation for evening use. Combined with grid-tie export, this gives you maximum flexibility and the best possible return on your investment.

🚗 Portable Off-Grid Units

Our smaller portable SGT units attach to standard car roof racks for complete mobility. Perfect for camping, agricultural use, remote sites, or anywhere you need power without a grid connection.

📊 Energy Monitoring

Every SGT comes with smart monitoring capability, allowing you to track your generation, consumption, and export in real time via your smartphone. Know exactly how much your system is earning at any moment.

🔧 Simple Installation

The SGT's lightweight 45kg design means no heavy groundworks or complex foundations are required. A qualified electrician can complete a full installation in a single day, dramatically reducing installation costs versus traditional tracker systems.
