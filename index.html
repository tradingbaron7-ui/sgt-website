The Energy Challenge

Fixed solar panels waste 40-60% of available sunlight. Traditional panels face one direction all day while the sun moves across the sky. They overheat in summer, losing efficiency. Existing tracking systems cost £15,000+ putting them out of reach for most people.

Our Solution: Like a real sunflower, our SGT tracks the sun on two axes throughout the day. Integrated water cooling keeps panels at optimal temperature while generating hot water. 10 curved petals fold for transport and deploy to capture maximum energy - all at a fraction of the cost of traditional trackers.
